# Retro Diner Poster complete creative prompt

```text
Create ONE highly polished vintage American RETRO DINER food-blog recipe graphic with distinctive custom lettering and energetic food-first composition.

EXACT RECIPE TITLE: "{{TITLE}}"

ORIGINAL PHOTO PROTECTION:
Use the uploaded original photograph as the authentic single food photograph. Do not regenerate food, add sauces/toppings, duplicate dishes, modify serving props, change colors, or introduce artificial gloss. Preserve the actual meal, plate, background, lighting and realistic imperfections. Graphics and typography are built outside the protected photograph.

DESIGN VISION:
Capture the cheerful confidence of classic American diners, family-run pizza parlors, old takeout menus and mid-century comfort-food graphics. This must feel visually bold, friendly and professional—NOT a generic modern magazine cover, nor a duplicate of the Black Label Western sign.

COMPOSITION:
Create a dynamic layout with one large hero food photograph, a clever retro framing silhouette, and an assertive title area that does not obscure important food. Consider a curved diner-menu header, compact marquee label, offset ticket tab, angled typography section or photo-led composition with a slim contrasting title strip. Choose ONE coherent approach intelligently. Work with the photo's shape rather than always centering a square image inside a frame. Avoid huge blank areas and repetitive geometry.

RETRO COLOR IDENTITY:
Use vintage cherry red, warm ivory, muted navy or forest green, mustard-gold, and black/dark cocoa in a controlled, food-appropriate combination. The exact selection is guided by the real food image. Checkerboard details may be red-and-cream or dark-cream, used sparingly; do not turn the entire poster into checkered fabric.

HANDCRAFTED GRAPHICS:
Use distinctive rounded vintage badges or menu-shaped frames, graceful arches, fine retro stripes, thin contrasting rules, small starburst flourishes and lightly worn offset-print textures. Decoration must be intentionally placed, not randomly repeated. The graphic should feel like an authentic printed American diner special with real dimensional paper/ink character.

TITLE DESIGN:
Show the EXACT words "{{TITLE}}" and nothing else. Choose a confident mid-century display serif, bold condensed slab, or carefully paired vintage diner type family with distinctive personality. Build one strong primary phrase and supporting words, fitting the title with measured proportions rather than tiny body text. Perfectly readable when viewed on a phone. No invented slogans, menu prices, sale prices or restaurant brand names.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT:
The graphic language is energetic and playful, unlike the botanical Farmhouse card, ornate Black Label sign, ruled Notebook card or restrained Old Cookbook print. It should have lively shapes and confident rhythm, not simply different colors on the same photo+frame+title grid.

STRICTLY AVOID:
AI-generated replacement food, excessive artificial food gloss, generic cream rectangle title, heavy Western saloon gold borders, botanical gingham bow decoration, notebook ruled paper, modern flashy neon, large experimental magazine layouts, collages, extra ingredient text or watermarks.

FINAL:
ONE beautifully art-directed Retro Diner recipe graphic, 4:5, designed for final 1200 × 1500 PNG output. Keep the original photo the hero and the title legible.
```

## Direct image-edit rules

```text
DIRECT IMAGE EDIT RULES
- Produce ONE independent recipe graphic per request, not a collage or multi-panel variations.
- Treat the uploaded original food photograph as the authoritative photographic source.
- Do not invent or replace the food, garnish, utensils, dishware, textures or background within the source photograph.
- In the direct image-edit workflow, send the owner's food photograph as image 1. The image model creates the complete finished design; no later compositor adds the food or title.
- Keep the meal visually dominant. Place type and ornaments on a designed label or frame without hiding important food.
- Use the title string exactly as supplied: {{TITLE}}. If NO TEXT is selected, render no text.
- No invented ingredients, preparation times, ratings, health claims, logos, URLs, or slogans.
- Style variation must be material in hierarchy, typography, graphic treatment and geometry, not only colors.
- The app exports one 1200 × 1500 PNG. Keep the food and complete title within a centered 4:5 safe area because the selected model's native portrait size may differ.
- Direct image edits cannot verify identical source pixels. Compare the finished result with the original photo before publishing.
- Honor user-selected model/quality, budget checks and explicit paid-call consent.
```
