# Recipe photo editor

Read the canonical [photo profile](../../../lib/recipe-intelligence/photoProfile.ts). `PHOTO_INSTRUCTIONS`, `ANALYSIS_INSTRUCTIONS`, `photoPayload` and their version constants control the worker. Keep changes there; do not maintain a second runtime prompt inside this skill.

Before estimating or generating, capture only photographs belonging to the current recipe: its ingredients, preparation, cooking steps and finished dish. Exclude related posts, recommended recipes, other-article cards, advertisements, author portraits, logos and app badges. A shared ingredient or a similar dish does not make another recipe's image eligible.

Determine ownership from the main article container, all ancestor recommendation markers, image-link destinations and metadata matching the current recipe. Keep legitimate same-page and full-size-image links, lazy-loaded cooking photos and recipe hero images. Deduplicate responsive sizes and crops; never fill a photo quota with unrelated images. Apply this during free discovery, before automatic selection and the cost estimate, without adding a paid filtering stage. Maintain this rule in the [Chrome capture code](../../../extensions/recipe-intelligence-capture/content.js) and [URL photo discovery](../../../lib/recipe-intelligence/imports.ts). Existing imports and approved job snapshots retain their saved photos; changing this skill does not rewrite them.

Review the paired [approved examples](../../../tests/fixtures/approved-recipe-photos/README.md) for food identity and cooking-stage fidelity. The pasta remains in a frying pan. The chili sequence retains distinct pale-onion, spiced-onion, clear-broth, chicken-added, creamy and served stages. These older examples do not establish approval of v5 compositions or rights to third-party photographs.

Use photo profile v5 with understanding profile v3. Aim for an everyday home-kitchen photograph as if taken with an **iPhone 17 Pro Max in normal Photo mode**: natural available light, neutral food colors, plausible reflections and focus, and irregular food and skin texture. Avoid waxy chicken, plastic-looking sauce, synthetic gloss, repeated textures, excessive smoothing/sharpening, HDR halos, fake portrait blur and studio-ad polish. The phone name is a style reference, never text to add to the image. Inspect future approved test results; mocked provider tests cannot establish visual quality.

Separate fixed recipe facts from photographic styling. Preserve visible ingredients and preparation forms, pasta shape, cut size, approximate quantities and proportions, sauce color/consistency, texture, doneness and exact stage. Preserve meaningful food arrangements such as layers, toppings and unmixed ingredients. Do not invent garnish or unseen fillings to support a new angle.

Compose the scene anew: vary framing, vessel position, negative space, existing prop relationships and a modest camera angle where the evidenced action remains clear. Ingredient flat lays can remain overhead while their layout changes. Use plain, unbranded cookware in the same category, structural material, overall shape and approximate capacity, retaining functional handles and utensil roles. Design details and finishes may differ. Keep the same recurring design and palette across the recipe, with a simple surface and soft side-window light. A skillet remains a skillet and a cooking pot remains a cooking pot; do not plate food from a cooking-stage photo. Do not add props, hands, side dishes, ingredient bowls or labels to cooking/served photos. Modest redistribution within the vessel is allowed only when food identity and meaningful cooking arrangements stay intact.

Apply the palette consistently to every relevant cooking/serving panel, with distinct realistic vessel and utensil finishes. Wood can change stain/tone while retaining grain, and metal utensils can change metallic finish while retaining reflections. Keep clear glass transparent and exposed uncoated metal cooking surfaces metallic. Defaults remain muted blue cookware, dark walnut utensils and a warm beige surface, configurable and frozen per recipe. A recolor, mirror, crop or new collage grid alone is not a newly composed scene. Preserve each collage panel's stage, action and chronological order.

Do not frame these changes as avoiding detection or copyright clearance. Prefer owned or appropriately licensed references, or independent generation based on recipe facts. The reference workflow still sends source-image bytes to the edit endpoint; v5 does not establish rights to them or guarantee that the output is independent enough for publication. Keep provenance and review results before publishing.

Ingredient photos preserve every ingredient and preparation group, including separate cheese/bean varieties. Require a visibly different arrangement of the existing containers and their contents: move the main container to a different side/row and exchange other groups so their relative positions and neighbors change. A wider crop, whole-layout shift/rotation/mirror, background recolor or new labels does not count as rearrangement. Keep every container, ingredient and group present; do not combine or split them. Replace floating captions with small paper labels that move with the correct containers. A shared seasoning dish may have one card listing its supported names. Exclude decorative footnotes and asterisks from labels; include confidently identified visible groups even if the original lacks a caption. Uncertain names require review for that photo only.

Use each photo's own original bytes at the Images edit endpoint. Default: Sunburst 2.6, high, 1536 × 864, opaque, auto moderation, no partial previews, omitted complexity. Compose for landscape proportionally without stretching food or cropping away ingredient groups. Preserve other explicit supported settings and never silently switch models.

Original blogger photographs are reference inputs only. They may appear in the clearly labeled original/edited comparison for review, but never as images in the final article, WordPress copy, or recipe-card image fields. Insert only saved generated/edited outputs. If an edit is missing, failed or unreadable, omit it and retain its retry status; never fall back to the original photograph or its source URL.

Use the application workflow: free recipe-only discovery and deduplication; all eligible photos auto-selected with optional manual changes; estimate selected photos plus cached GPT-5.5 high-detail analysis in groups of ten or fewer; explicit job budget approval; independent image stages and targeted retries. There is no three-photo selection threshold. An earlier test's spending approval does not authorize a new job.

Save provider receipts before exports. Repair from receipts without a new paid call. Keep uncertain charges reserved. Preserve successful outputs and article text. Use saved approved files and mocked responses for implementation tests, with no paid generation. Increment the application profile version when editing the instructions and retain frozen versions on existing jobs.
