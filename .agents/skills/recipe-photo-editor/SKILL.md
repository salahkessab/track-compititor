# Recipe photo editor

Read the canonical [photo profile](../../../lib/recipe-intelligence/photoProfile.ts). `PHOTO_INSTRUCTIONS`, `ANALYSIS_INSTRUCTIONS`, `photoPayload` and their version constants control the worker. Keep changes there; do not maintain a second runtime prompt inside this skill.

Before estimating or generating, capture only photographs belonging to the current recipe: its ingredients, preparation, cooking steps and finished dish. Exclude related posts, recommended recipes, other-article cards, advertisements, author portraits, logos and app badges. A shared ingredient or a similar dish does not make another recipe's image eligible.

Determine ownership from the main article container, all ancestor recommendation markers, image-link destinations and metadata matching the current recipe. Keep legitimate same-page and full-size-image links, lazy-loaded cooking photos and recipe hero images. Deduplicate responsive sizes and crops; never fill a photo quota with unrelated images. Apply this during free discovery, before automatic selection and the cost estimate, without adding a paid filtering stage. Maintain this rule in the [Chrome capture code](../../../extensions/recipe-intelligence-capture/content.js) and [URL photo discovery](../../../lib/recipe-intelligence/imports.ts). Existing imports and approved job snapshots retain their saved photos; changing this skill does not rewrite them.

Review the paired [approved examples](../../../tests/fixtures/approved-recipe-photos/README.md). The pasta remains in its frying pan. The chili sequence retains distinct pale-onion, spiced-onion, clear-broth, chicken-added, creamy and served stages. Never transfer the food into another vessel.

Use the current v4 application profile. Aim for an everyday home-kitchen photograph as if taken with an **iPhone 17 Pro Max in normal Photo mode**: natural available light, neutral food colors, plausible reflections and focus, and preserved irregular food and skin texture. Avoid waxy chicken, plastic-looking sauce, synthetic gloss, repeated textures, excessive smoothing/sharpening, HDR halos, fake portrait blur and studio-ad polish. The phone name is a style reference, never text to add to the image. Prompt wording does not guarantee a perfectly realistic result; inspect the result when testing a new profile.

Cooking and served photos preserve food, quantities, texture, consistency, stage, vessel category, shape, structural material, handles, utensil shape/function, food arrangement, props, hands and camera angle. Do not add bowls, ingredient spreads, measuring jugs or paper labels to these photos. Ingredient-layout and label rules apply only to ingredient photos. Extend only the existing surface or stove when widening a cooking shot.

Visibly change the original serving plate/pan/pot/bowl, spoon/utensil and surface colors separately, including every relevant collage panel. A tiny grey-to-blue-grey shift, changed background or new collage grid is insufficient. If the selected color is close to the original, use a clearly contrasting stronger/lighter/darker shade in that color family. Keep that chosen finish consistent for the same recurring objects across the recipe; never recolor food or skin. Preserve clear glass and bare-metal material exceptions rather than forcing an unrealistic recolor.

Material preservation does not mean preserving the old color: a wooden spoon may change wood stain/tone while retaining grain, and a metal utensil may change metallic finish while retaining reflections. Keep clear glass transparent and exposed uncoated metal cooking surfaces metallic. Never replace the vessel or utensil with a different type. Defaults are muted blue cookware, dark walnut utensils and a warm beige surface, all configurable and frozen per recipe.

Ingredient photos preserve every ingredient and preparation group, including separate cheese/bean varieties. Require a visibly different arrangement of the existing containers and their contents: move the main container to a different side/row and exchange other groups so their relative positions and neighbors change. A wider crop, whole-layout shift/rotation/mirror, background recolor or new labels does not count as rearrangement. Keep every container, ingredient and group present; do not combine or split them. Replace floating captions with small paper labels that move with the correct containers. A shared seasoning dish may have one card listing its supported names. Exclude decorative footnotes and asterisks from labels; include confidently identified visible groups even if the original lacks a caption. Uncertain names require review for that photo only.

Use each photo's own original bytes at the Images edit endpoint. Default: Sunburst 2.6, high, 1536 × 864, opaque, auto moderation, no partial previews, omitted complexity. Extend the surface proportionally for landscape framing without stretching food. Preserve other explicit supported settings and never silently switch models.

Original blogger photographs are reference inputs only. They may appear in the clearly labeled original/edited comparison for review, but never as images in the final article, WordPress copy, or recipe-card image fields. Insert only saved generated/edited outputs. If an edit is missing, failed or unreadable, omit it and retain its retry status; never fall back to the original photograph or its source URL.

Use the application workflow: free recipe-only discovery and deduplication; all eligible photos auto-selected with optional manual changes; estimate selected photos plus cached GPT-5.5 high-detail analysis in groups of ten or fewer; explicit job budget approval; independent image stages and targeted retries. There is no three-photo selection threshold. An earlier test's spending approval does not authorize a new job.

Save provider receipts before exports. Repair from receipts without a new paid call. Keep uncertain charges reserved. Preserve successful outputs and article text. Use saved approved files and mocked responses for implementation tests, with no paid generation. Increment the application profile version when editing the instructions and retain frozen versions on existing jobs.
