# Black Label Vintage complete creative prompt

```text
Create ONE exceptional professional American food-blog recipe graphic in a dramatic PREMIUM VINTAGE BLACK LABEL style.

EXACT RECIPE TITLE: "{{TITLE}}"

DESIGN GOAL:
Produce a visually rich nostalgic American diner or Southern restaurant sign composition built around the authentic uploaded photograph. This must feel like engraved antique packaging and real vintage sign painting, not a black rectangle with one default font. The design should instantly communicate old restaurant charm, expressive typography and handcrafted craftsmanship.

AUTHENTIC ORIGINAL FOOD PHOTO:
Preserve the uploaded photo's exact food details, ingredient positions, dishware, camera angle, lighting, surface texture and natural imperfections. Never regenerate, relight, recolor, invent toppings, swap the food scene or create extra side dishes. All graphics must be constructed around the protected original photo; it remains the largest visual element.

OVERALL COMPOSITION:
Use an engaging photo-first vintage sign layout. Let the photo occupy most of the design, with an authentic ornamental black recipe label placed in a position that respects important food visibility. The label could be a compact lower-corner shield, a distinctive arched plaque, a narrow banner below the photograph, or a custom angled ticket: choose a design that fits the photo/title and is distinct from the last design. Do not default to one huge black plaque occupying the lower third. The photo and black sign must interact through visual rhythm, clean silhouette and exact spacing without obscuring food.

COLORS:
Rich ink-black and aged charcoal (#211E1A), warm antique cream (#F3E5C7), weathered mustard-gold (#C49A4B), muted barn red/burgundy (#8B3028), and just a touch of tobacco brown. Customize small accents to coordinate with the photograph, but retain recognizable vintage label contrast. Do not recolor source food.

ORNAMENTAL GRAPHIC QUALITY:
Build elegant double- or triple-line antique-gold border work, genuinely crafted mitered/curved corners, embossed or engraved flourishes, controlled distressed ink/grain, refined separators and restrained star emblems. Make the label a high-quality signboard with real ornament relationships, NOT an automatic rectangular border and four tiny generic diamonds. Allow subtle letterpress printing imperfections around sign edges.

PREMIUM DISPLAY TYPOGRAPHY:
Render ONLY "{{TITLE}}". Use authoritative vintage slab-serif, condensed engraved serif, Western/Americana diner letterforms as fits the dish, with excellent visual proportions. Establish custom hierarchy: a compact introductory line, a dominant title phrase, and a balanced secondary phrase when length permits. Full wording must be preserved exactly; dynamic wrapping should not miss or reorder words. Use antique cream and muted gold lettering with restrained red highlights. Typography should feel like it was drawn for this label, professionally kerning and spacing each line. Maintain full readability on a mobile Facebook feed.

VISUAL INTENT:
The food photo looks authentic and homemade while the labels and framing look rich, aged, premium and dramatic. This is a traditional food-blog recipe graphic, not a modern magazine cover or a fake restaurant brand advertisement.

STRICTLY AVOID:
Simple flat black strips, generic geometric rectangles, tiny unstyled titles, endless empty borders, excessive overlapping labels, food reconstruction, AI-created garnishes, giant typography blocking the dish, modern glossy 3D, unrelated food photos, ingredient lists, fake restaurant logos, slogans or watermarks.

FINAL OUTPUT:
ONE polished, expertly art-directed, black-label American comfort-food graphic. Authentic food photo dominant. Strong signboard workmanship and exceptional distressed display type. Intended final export 1200 × 1500 PNG, vertical 4:5.
```
