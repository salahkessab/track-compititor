# Notebook Comfort Food complete creative prompt

```text
Create ONE beautiful, sophisticated Facebook food-blog graphic in the style of a cherished vintage American RECIPE NOTEBOOK page.

EXACT RECIPE TITLE: "{{TITLE}}"

MISSION:
Give the real uploaded food photograph a charming, nostalgic recipe-journal presentation that feels human, homemade, and professionally designed. The result should resemble a delightful old family recipe pasted into a real notebook, not an empty beige background or a plain centered index card.

PHOTO PROTECTION:
The uploaded photograph supplies the actual food content. Preserve ingredients, food portions, dishware, toppings, lighting, natural imperfections and original photographic character. Do not redraw food, add props, create a replacement scene or alter the original source. Keep the food photo as the focal point and design all paper/tape/ornament work around it.

DESIGN LANGUAGE:
Use warm lined notebook paper, ivory stationery, softly aged edges, realistic paper fibers, muted red and navy/olive recipe-book inks, and decorative hand-finished details. The texture must resemble tangible notebook paper and authentic printed stationery, not a flat cream fill.

DYNAMIC COMPOSITION:
Place one large authentic food image in the upper or central region. Integrate a natural recipe-note label below or to one side, depending on title length and photo proportions. Make the label look genuinely crafted: narrow paper margins, fine faint ruled lines, a subtle red margin rule, carefully placed gingham washi tape, occasional notebook binding details, and small hand-drawn botanical ornaments. Avoid clipping or obstructing the food. Choose a fresh balance of paper/photo for each recipe, with no excessive dead space.

TITLE ART DIRECTION:
Write the exact title "{{TITLE}}", without any other copy unless separately supplied. Use a meaningful typographic hierarchy with old-fashioned bold cookbook serif for the main words and a restrained typewriter/hand-lettered accent for smaller connective words when helpful. Deep country burgundy, muted olive, navy and warm cocoa are appropriate ink choices. Give the headline strong visual presence; do not use the basic website UI font at an arbitrary size. Ensure accurate capitalization/spelling and easy mobile reading.

DECORATIVE DETAILS:
Choose two or three: authentic red-and-white gingham tape, ruled writing lines, tiny floral corner prints, small stitching marks, a modest recipe-journal tab, or fine hand-drawn separators. Add subtle shadows from layered paper. These must visually integrate as a real crafted notebook spread, not random clip art. Avoid excessive hearts and fake hand-drawn ingredients.

QUALITY STANDARD:
This should resemble a highly shareable heritage home-cooking recipe-card graphic. Photo first; charming note/recipe title second. Tactile, expressive, beautifully balanced and polished.

DO NOT:
Invent ingredients, bake times, recipe notes or slogans. Do not generate new food, include multiple photos, produce a collage, overdecorate, use giant magazine-style typography, make a plain centered photo with narrow border or reuse the Black Label treatment.

FINAL OUTPUT:
ONE refined Notebook Comfort Food recipe design, 4:5 vertical, intended final output 1200 × 1500 PNG.
```

## Direct image-edit rules

```text
DIRECT IMAGE EDIT RULES
- Produce ONE independent recipe graphic per request, not a collage or multi-panel variations.
- Treat the uploaded original food photograph as the authoritative photographic source.
- Do not invent or replace the food, garnish, utensils, dishware, textures or background within the source photograph.
- In the direct image-edit workflow, send the owner's food photograph as image 1. The image model creates the complete finished design; no later compositor adds the food or title.
- Keep the meal visually dominant. Place type and ornaments on a designed label or frame without hiding important food.
- Use the title string exactly as supplied: {{TITLE}}. If NO TEXT is selected, render no text.
- No invented ingredients, preparation times, ratings, health claims, logos, URLs, or slogans.
- Style variation must be material in hierarchy, typography, graphic treatment and geometry, not only colors.
- The app exports one 1200 × 1500 PNG. Keep the food and complete title within a centered 4:5 safe area because the selected model's native portrait size may differ.
- Direct image edits cannot verify identical source pixels. Compare the finished result with the original photo before publishing.
- Honor user-selected model/quality, budget checks and explicit paid-call consent.
```
