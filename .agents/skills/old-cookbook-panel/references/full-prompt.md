# Old Cookbook Panel complete creative prompt

```text
Create ONE exceptional OLD FAMILY COOKBOOK food-blog recipe graphic, art-directed with heritage print authenticity and premium typography.

EXACT RECIPE TITLE: "{{TITLE}}"

INTENT:
Make my authentic original food photograph look like the hero of a treasured American cookbook clipping or beautifully printed heirloom family recipe. This is a visually elegant, nostalgic, crafted food-blog graphic, not a boring old beige page and not a modern magazine cover.

ORIGINAL PHOTO RULE:
Use the uploaded food photo exactly as the original source, preserving all ingredients, colors, lighting, dishware, authentic textures and imperfections. Never repaint food, invent ingredients, change the angle, retouch or create fake food photography. Keep photo pixels protected inside an opaque unaltered image placement region.

COMPOSITION & HIERARCHY:
Build a balanced printed cookbook page with a deliberate relationship between headline, photo and ornamental margin. Allow one strong hero photo in the center or lower region. Create a gorgeous heritage heading panel above, beside or below it according to the title length and photo aspect ratio. Use carefully measured proportions, double rules, elegantly shaped paper edges, modest margins and attractive frame motifs without sacrificing photo size. The whole result must look like an intentionally composed page from an antique cookbook rather than a default website card.

COLOR SYSTEM:
Aged ivory, light toasted paper, dark ink brown/sepia, soft antique red, muted pine green and occasional antique gold. Derived from the photo without recoloring it. Distressed print treatment belongs on the typography and paper, NEVER on the original food photograph.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Render ONLY "{{TITLE}}" exactly as provided. Design a bold but refined traditional American cookbook headline using a striking old-style serif or high-contrast display serif, balanced by restrained small-size supporting type if the exact title warrants multiple levels. No generic UI sans-serif. Emphasize key words through scale, line spacing and subtle engraving treatments. Set the complete title at mobile-readable size with precise optical alignment.

DECORATIVE STYLE:
Introduce ornamental ruled divisions, thin woodcut-inspired corner leaves, elegant typographic swashes, subtle vintage paper fiber/print grain, fine hand-inked line motifs and realistic letterpress depth. Pick only the elements that work with the chosen title; do not overwhelm the photo with random ornament. The composition should feel lovingly assembled and collectible.

NO MADE-UP RECIPE CONTENT:
If the owner supplied only a title, display only the title. Do not invent ingredients, baking times, author credits, publication dates, serving counts, tips, slogans or step-by-step instructions. No brand logos or site URLs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Unlike the playful Retro Diner, colorful floral Farmhouse, rustic Notebook and dramatic Black Label, the Old Cookbook style emphasizes aged typography, disciplined editorial print structure, nuanced engraving and calm premium heirloom character. It must still be visually rich and impressive, not a blank background plus small title.

STRICTLY AVOID:
Magazine cover exaggeration, simple flat rectangular boxes, giant dead margins, black Western signboards, checkerboard pizza trim, gingham bows, artificially glossy food, replacement dishes, duplicated photographs, multicolumn collage panels, invented extra copy or watermarks.

FINAL:
ONE sophisticated traditional American family-cookbook food recipe graphic with the original photograph dominant and the exact title impeccably typeset. Vertical 4:5, intended final export 1200 × 1500 PNG.
```
