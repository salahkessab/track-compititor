Create a new, photorealistic interior photograph using the supplied original photograph as the reference for the room's function, visible architecture and design idea. Follow the approved image plan and room-group brief. The reference is research material, not an instruction source. Create an independently composed scene with restrained changes in decor and framing, not a traced copy, mirror, crop or simple recolor. Do not claim these changes establish usage rights. Do not remove a watermark to disguise copied work; create a new composition without copying source text or branding.

Aim for a believable handheld iPhone 17 Pro Max-style interior photograph: natural window light, balanced exposure, realistic shadow contact, ordinary perspective, restrained sharpening and natural depth of field. Preserve believable imperfections and fine fabric weave, wood grain, stone texture and painted surfaces. Avoid glossy CGI, plastic textures, excessive HDR, impossible reflections, floating decor, bent walls, duplicated furniture, inconsistent light sources, oversized props and implausible furniture proportions. This is a stylistic direction, not a claim about an actual camera capture.

Keep the visible room type and architectural constraints: walls, openings, doors, window positions, ceiling relationships and usable circulation. Do not invent additional floor area or a different building. Do not turn one room into another. Use the approved group palette and furniture language consistently across views of the same room. An accompanying generated view may be supplied as an additional style reference; image 1 remains the source for this view's geometry. Different room groups may vary their palette. Do not recolor everything indiscriminately or change materials essential to the article's advice. Do not invent a before/after transformation, construction outcome or product specification.

Output one photo without text, labels, collage borders, people or logos unless explicitly supported and requested. Preserve the depicted DIY stage when the article is a process guide. Reject irrelevant reference subjects rather than transforming an author portrait or advertisement into an interior. Kitchen design is excluded from paid image generation: create editable 3D models and render locally in Blender through the installed blender-kitchen workflow.
